### 3. Description of the Service

Civic Voice Canada is a civic transparency application that collects, organises, and
displays publicly available information about the Canadian federal government. This includes
information about Members of Parliament, Senators, Cabinet Ministers, government legislation,
federal contracts and spending, departmental budgets, and related matters of public interest.

The App is intended to help Canadians explore how their federal government works, access
information that is already in the public domain, and form their own views about public
affairs.

You can browse public civic information in the App without creating an account, unless
optional account features are made available in the future and you choose to use them.

### 8. Data Accuracy, Freshness, and Availability

We take reasonable care to display accurate and reasonably current information, but we
cannot guarantee it.

In particular:

- **Official data may be delayed.** Government datasets are published on their own
  schedules. The App may not reflect the most recent update.
- **Data may be incomplete.** Some information is not publicly disclosed, is redacted in
  official sources, or is not available in a machine-readable format.
- **Data may be transformed.** Some figures in the App are grouped, summarised, calculated,
  or formatted from source data. Where this is the case, the App identifies the source and
  describes the transformation where practical.
- **Data may be temporarily unavailable.** Source APIs, websites, or datasets may be
  offline, changed, or blocked, in which case the App will display an honest label such as
  "Source unavailable" or "Official data not loaded yet" rather than a stale or incorrect
  figure.
- **Data may contain errors.** Official government datasets occasionally contain errors.
  We will correct data in the App when errors are identified and verified. See Section 11
  for how to submit a correction request.

We do not guarantee that any information in the App is complete, current, error-free, or
suitable for any particular purpose.

### 9. User Responsibility to Verify Original Sources

For any decision, action, or reliance that matters to you, **please verify information
directly with the original official source**.

Key Canadian official sources include:

- Parliament of Canada: [parl.ca](https://www.parl.ca)
- Elections Canada: [elections.ca](https://www.elections.ca)
- Government of Canada Open Data: [open.canada.ca](https://open.canada.ca)
- Public Accounts of Canada: available via the Office of the Comptroller General
- CanadaBuys (Government Contracts): [canadabuys.canada.ca](https://canadabuys.canada.ca)

Civic Voice Canada is a starting point for civic exploration, not a substitute for
consulting primary sources.
