## 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard public disclaimer and non-affiliation wording for
Civic Voice Canada.

Its goals are to:

- Clearly communicate to users that Civic Voice Canada is an independent platform with no
  government, political, or official affiliation.
- Protect Civic Voice Canada from reputational, legal, or regulatory risk arising from
  users misunderstanding the app's status, authority, or scope.
- Provide ready-to-use, pre-approved wording for every context where a disclaimer is
  required — app UI, App Store listing, data source pages, charts, modals, and legal documents.
- Ensure the app never overpromises accuracy, authority, completeness, or official status.
- Support compliance with the Open Government Licence – Canada (OGL-Canada 2.0)
  non-endorsement requirements and with App Store and Play Store review guidelines.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all public-facing surfaces of Civic Voice Canada, including:

- The mobile and web app UI (all screens, footers, modals, cards, charts)
- The App Store and Google Play listing, support URL, and privacy URL
- The public Data Sources page (civicvoice.ca/sources — TBD)
- The Terms of Use (CV-POL-003)
- The Privacy Policy (CV-POL-001)
- Any press release, blog post, or social media content published under the Civic Voice
  Canada brand
- Any in-app copy that describes what the app is, what the data means, or what users
  can do with it

This policy does not govern communications with government bodies, legal filings, or
internal documentation — those are covered by the relevant policies.

---

## 3. Standard Short Disclaimer

The following is the approved **short disclaimer** for use in UI contexts where space is
limited — footers, card subtitles, tab bar descriptions, and similar compact locations.

> **Civic Voice Canada is an independent public-information app. It is not affiliated
> with, endorsed by, or operated by any government body, public agency, political party,
> elected official, or candidate.**

**Usage notes:**

- Use verbatim. Do not shorten further unless discussed with the Compliance Lead.
- This wording is suitable for display in a small-text footer, an "About" chip, or a
  data attribution subtitle.
- It does not substitute for the full disclaimer in contexts where the full disclaimer
  is required — see Section 11.

---

## 4. Standard Full Disclaimer

The following is the approved **full disclaimer** for use in the About page, Terms of
Use, Privacy Policy, Data Sources page, and any context where a complete statement of
the app's independence and scope is required.

> **Civic Voice Canada is an independent civic transparency and public-information
> application. The app organizes and displays Canadian public-sector, public registry,
> and open-government information for general informational, civic-awareness,
> transparency, and educational purposes only.**
>
> **Civic Voice Canada is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated
> by the Government of Canada, any provincial or territorial government, municipality,
> public agency, political party, elected official, candidate, or public office holder.**
>
> **Information shown in the app may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, transformed,
> summarized, or subject to manual review. Users should verify important information
> directly with the original official source.**

**Usage notes:**

- Use verbatim. Paragraph breaks may be adjusted for UI layout.
- This wording must appear in the About page, Terms of Use, and Data Sources page.
- It may be preceded or followed by source-specific attribution wording — see Section 6.

## 5. Footer Disclaimer

The following is the approved **footer disclaimer** for the app's persistent footer,
web footer, and any screen-level footer where a brief statement is shown.

> Civic Voice Canada — Independent public-information app.
> Not affiliated with any government body, agency, or political party.

**Usage notes:**

- This is a condensed footer form. It is suitable for a persistent UI footer where a
  single line or two-line statement is needed.
- It must link to the About page or Data Sources page where the full disclaimer is shown.
- The footer must also include a link to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

## 6. Data Sources Page Disclaimer

The following wording must appear prominently at the top of the public Data Sources
page (civicvoice.ca/sources — TBD) and in any in-app Data Sources screen.

> **About this data**
>
> Civic Voice Canada organizes and displays publicly available Canadian government and
> public-sector information for civic-awareness and transparency purposes. All factual
> data shown in this app is sourced from official government open-data sources, public
> registries, or other official publications listed below.
>
> Some information may be delayed, incomplete, or subject to manual review. Where data
> has been grouped, summarised, or calculated from a source dataset, this is noted in
> the source entry below.
>
> Civic Voice Canada is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated
> by any government body or agency. Use of government data does not imply endorsement by
> the source organization.
>
> Contains information licensed under applicable Canadian open-government licences,
> including the Open Government Licence – Canada (OGL-Canada 2.0).
> [open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada)
>
> For authoritative and current information, always verify with the original official
> source linked in each entry below.

**Usage notes:**

- This wording must appear before the source list on the Data Sources page.
- It may be placed in a card, callout box, or section header — styling is flexible.
- The OGL-Canada attribution line is required and must not be removed.

## 7. Province / Territory Page Disclaimer

The following wording must appear on any page or section that displays information about
public officials (MPs, Senators, Cabinet Ministers, or other named individuals in public
roles) or public records associated with a specific province or territory.

> **About this information**
>
> The profiles and records shown here are sourced from official Canadian government
> publications and open-data sources. They reflect publicly available information about
> individuals in public office or public roles.
>
> Civic Voice Canada does not make findings of corruption, misconduct, or unlawful
> conduct. It does not rank, score, or rate public officials. It does not make voting
> recommendations or candidate endorsements.
>
> All information shown may be delayed or incomplete. Verify with the original official
> source before relying on it.

**Usage notes:**

- This wording may be shown as a collapsible "About this data" section on province or
  territory pages to avoid visual clutter.
- It must be accessible within one tap or click from any public official profile card.
- It does not need to appear on every individual card — once per page or section is sufficient.

## 8. Modal / Chart Disclaimer

The following wording must appear in any modal, tooltip, or chart view where data has
been transformed, summarised, grouped, or calculated from an official source.

> **Source note**
>
> This chart [or: this figure / these totals] is derived from [SOURCE NAME], [REPORTING
> PERIOD], fetched [DATE]. [TRANSFORMATION NOTE — e.g., "Records grouped by department.
> Percentages calculated by Civic Voice Canada."]
>
> Civic Voice Canada is independent and is not endorsed by [SOURCE AGENCY].

**Usage notes:**

- Replace the bracketed placeholders with the values from CV-REG-001 for the relevant
  dataset.
- "Transformation note" is required whenever data has been grouped, aggregated,
  calculated, or filtered — see CV-POL-002 Section 10 for the full transformation
  documentation requirement.
- If no transformation has been applied, the transformation note line may be omitted.
- This wording applies to charts, spending breakdowns, vote totals, aggregate counts,
  and any other derived figure.

## 9. App Store Disclaimer

The following wording must appear in the App Store (Apple) and Google Play listing
description, support URL page, and/or privacy URL page. It may be adapted to fit
character limits, but the key claims must be preserved.

**Short form (for character-limited descriptions):**

> Civic Voice Canada is an independent civic transparency app. Not affiliated with the
> Government of Canada or any government body.

**Full form (for support/privacy page):**

> **About Civic Voice Canada**
>
> Civic Voice Canada is an independent civic transparency and public-information
> application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government
> of Canada, any provincial or territorial government, or any political party, elected
> official, or candidate.
>
> The app organizes publicly available Canadian government and public-sector information
> for informational, civic-awareness, and educational purposes. Information shown may
> be delayed, incomplete, or subject to manual review. Users should verify important
> information directly with original official sources.
>
> Civic Voice Canada does not provide legal, financial, voting, or professional advice.
> It does not make voting recommendations, candidate endorsements, or corruption findings.
>
> Privacy Policy: [link]
> Terms of Use: [link]
> Data Sources: [link]

**Usage notes:**

- The full form must be published at the support URL before App Store submission.
- Apple App Store review guidelines require that the app's purpose and data handling be
  accurately described. Claims in the listing must match the app's actual functionality.
- The app must not be described in the listing as "official", "government", "verified
  by government", or similar — these phrases will trigger App Store rejection and are
  prohibited under Section 10 of this policy.

## 10. No-Advice and No-Recommendation Wording

The following wording must be available in the app's About or Legal section and in the
Terms of Use. It may also be used in modals or feature descriptions where civic-opinion
vote features are shown.

> **Civic Voice Canada does not provide legal, financial, investment, tax, voting,
> political, or professional advice. The app does not make voting recommendations,
> party recommendations, candidate endorsements, corruption findings, legal conclusions,
> or enforcement determinations.**

**Source verification note** (for use alongside data displays):

> Where practical, Civic Voice Canada provides source links, reporting periods, fetched
> dates, and transformation notes. Users should rely on the original official source for
> authoritative information.

**Open-data licence note** (for use on the Data Sources page and in legal documents):

> Some information may be used under applicable Canadian open-government licences or
> public registry terms. Use of government or public-sector information does not imply
> endorsement by the source organization.

## 11. Prohibited Claims

The following claims, descriptions, and phrases are **prohibited** in any public-facing
Civic Voice Canada context unless explicitly reviewed and approved in writing by the
Compliance Lead and, where relevant, legal counsel.

| Prohibited Phrase or Claim | Why It Is Prohibited |
|---|---|
| "Official government app" | False — Civic Voice is not a government product |
| "Government verified" | False — no government body verifies our content |
| "Real-time official truth" | Overpromises currency and official status |
| "Detects corruption" | Makes an enforcement or legal finding we are not qualified to make |
| "Corruption score" | Implies an official or legal rating that the app does not produce |
| "Tells you who to vote for" | Makes a voting recommendation — prohibited under independence principles |
| "Best politician" / "Worst politician" | Makes a subjective rating or ranking that is not supported by official data |
| "Investment signal" | Constitutes financial or investment advice we are not licensed to give |
| "Legal determination" | Constitutes legal advice we are not qualified to give |
| "Enforcement finding" | Implies regulatory authority we do not hold |
| "Guaranteed accurate" | Overpromises accuracy of data that is sourced from third-party official sources |
| "Complete government record" | Overpromises completeness — government data is often partial or delayed |
| Anything implying endorsement by a named politician, party, or government body | Violates the OGL-Canada 2.0 non-endorsement condition and independence principles |
| Anything implying affiliation with Elections Canada, the Parliament of Canada, or any government agency | False and may mislead users about the nature of the app |

If a proposed feature, marketing message, or UI copy element would require using or
implying any of the above, it must be raised with the Compliance Lead before development
or publication.

## 12. Required Use Locations

The disclaimer wording defined in this policy must be used in the following locations.
Each location specifies which disclaimer version is required.

| Location | Required Disclaimer | Version |
|---|---|---|
| App footer (persistent, all screens) | Footer disclaimer | Section 5 |
| About page / About screen | Full disclaimer + no-advice wording | Sections 4, 10 |
| Data Sources page / screen | Data Sources page disclaimer | Section 6 |
| Terms of Use (CV-POL-003) | Full disclaimer + no-advice wording | Sections 4, 10 |
| Privacy Policy (CV-POL-001) | Short disclaimer (where relevant) | Section 3 |
| Province / territory pages (public official profiles or records) | Province/territory disclaimer | Section 7 |
| Charts and modals showing transformed or calculated data | Modal / chart disclaimer with source note | Section 8 |
| App Store listing (Apple App Store / Google Play) | App Store disclaimer — short form in description, full form at support URL | Section 9 |
| Any feature that shows citizen-opinion vote totals | No-advice wording (votes are not endorsements or findings) | Section 10 |
| Any press release, blog post, or social media post under the Civic Voice brand | Short disclaimer at minimum | Section 3 |

Locations marked as "Data Sources page", "About page", and "App Store support URL" are
required before App Store submission and before any public launch.

## 13. Review and Approval

### 13.1 Review Frequency

This policy and all disclaimer wording must be reviewed:

- Before public launch of Civic Voice Canada
- Whenever the app's core positioning, features, or data sources materially change
- Whenever a new category of public official, government body, or government dataset is
  added to the app
- Whenever a new disclaimer location or use case is identified that is not covered by
  this policy
- At least annually after public launch

### 13.2 Approval Process

Any change to the standard disclaimer wording in Sections 3–10 requires:

1. A tracked change in version control (git) with a descriptive commit message
2. Version number increment in the document metadata
3. Review by the Compliance Lead
4. Legal review if the change materially affects the non-affiliation, no-advice, or
   prohibited-claims wording
5. Update to the Change Log below

### 13.3 Propagation of Changes

When approved disclaimer wording changes, the Compliance Lead must:

1. Update this document (CV-POL-004)
2. Update CV-POL-003 (Terms of Use) where the full disclaimer appears
3. Notify the developer to update all in-app locations listed in Section 12
4. Update the App Store listing support page if App Store wording is affected
