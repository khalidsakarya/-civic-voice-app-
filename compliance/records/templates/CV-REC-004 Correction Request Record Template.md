> This is a blank template. Complete one record per correction request received.
> Retain completed records in `compliance/records/` according to CV-SOP-003 §9.
> Each completed record should be named CV-REC-004-YYYY-MM-DD-COR-[N].md.
> Do not record requester contact details in a way that violates CV-POL-001 (Privacy Policy).
