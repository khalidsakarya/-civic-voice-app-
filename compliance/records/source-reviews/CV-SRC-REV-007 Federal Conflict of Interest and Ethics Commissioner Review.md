## 1. Purpose

This record documents the source review for data published by the Office of the
Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner of Canada. This office is an independent
officer of Parliament responsible for administering the *Conflict of Interest Act*
(for public office holders) and the *Conflict of Interest Code for Members of the
House of Commons*.

This is the highest-risk source in the Civic Voice Canada federal dataset because:
- Examination reports name individual officials and contain findings of violations
- Displaying a finding inaccurately — or attributing a finding to the wrong official —
  could cause serious harm
- Data must be from the official Commissioner's website, not a secondary source
- Framing in the app must not editorialize beyond what the Commissioner's report states
