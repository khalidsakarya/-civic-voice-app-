## 1. Purpose

This record documents the source review for the Office of the Conflict of Interest and
Ethics Commissioner of Canada. This office is an independent officer of Parliament
responsible for administering the *Conflict of Interest Act* (for public office holders)
and the *Conflict of Interest Code for Members of the House of Commons* (for MPs).

The office publishes:
- **Public disclosure summaries** (annual, filed by designated public office holders)
- **Examination reports** (when the Commissioner investigates possible violations and issues a formal finding)
- **Advisory opinions** (general guidance, not naming specific violations)

**Why Manual Review Only:** Reports are available only as PDF and HTML on
ciec-ccie.parl.gc.ca. No structured data export exists. Every record must be
individually identified, extracted, verified against the official report, and reviewed
by a second reviewer before it can be used in the app. This cannot be automated.
