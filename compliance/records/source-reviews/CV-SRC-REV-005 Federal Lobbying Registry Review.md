## 1. Purpose

This record documents the source review for the Office of the Commissioner of Lobbying
of Canada's Registry of Lobbyists. This is a statutory public register under the
*Lobbying Act*. It contains registration filings from lobbyists disclosing their
clients, purposes, and communications with designated public office holders (DPOHs).

This is a high-risk source because:
- It names individual public officials (Designated Public Office Holders)
- It names individual registered lobbyists
- Errors in displaying lobbying relationships could harm reputations
- Data must be used accurately and attributed clearly to the official registry
