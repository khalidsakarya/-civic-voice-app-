## 1. Purpose

This record documents the source review for the Office of the Integrity Commissioner
of Ontario. The Integrity Commissioner is an independent officer of the Ontario
Legislature responsible for administering the *Members' Integrity Act, 1994* and
providing ethical guidance to Ontario MPPs and ministers.

The office publishes:
- **MPP public disclosure statements** (annual, financial interests and outside activities)
- **Inquiry reports** (when the Integrity Commissioner investigates a request and issues findings)
- **Annual reports** to the Ontario Legislature

**Why Manual Review Only:** Reports are available only as PDF and HTML on oico.on.ca.
No structured data export exists. Every record must be individually identified,
extracted, verified against the official report, and reviewed by a second reviewer
before it can be used in the app. This cannot be automated.

**Provincial parallel:** This source is the Ontario provincial equivalent of the
federal Conflict of Interest and Ethics Commissioner (CV-SRC-REV-007). It should be
held to the same highest-risk standard and Manual Review Only classification.
