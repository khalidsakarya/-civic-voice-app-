## 1. Purpose

This record documents the source review for data published by the Office of the
Integrity Commissioner of Ontario. The Integrity Commissioner is an independent officer
of the Ontario Legislature responsible for administering the *Members' Integrity Act,
1994* and related statutes. The office publishes MPP disclosure statements and formal
inquiry reports.
