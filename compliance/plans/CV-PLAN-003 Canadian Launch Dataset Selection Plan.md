## 1. Purpose

This plan defines the exact Canadian datasets, reporting periods, Firestore collection
targets, and app display locations that must be decided before Civic Voice Canada can
fetch, verify, and display official source data publicly.

Its goals are to:

- Force explicit product decisions about which datasets are in scope for launch vs
  deferred to post-launch.
- Create a single controlled list of dataset candidates with confirmed or pending
  selection status.
- Map each selected dataset to its Firestore collection and app display location before
  any data is written.
- Prevent partial or undefined dataset selections from reaching the app pipeline.
- Provide the Data Lead with a clear input to the CV-REC-001 verification process.
- Ensure the dataset scope is consistent with CV-REG-001 source decisions, CV-POL-002
  source policy, and the high-risk controls in CV-SRC-REV-001 through CV-SRC-REV-011.

---

## 2. Scope

This plan covers all 11 registered Canadian sources (SRC-FED-001 through SRC-FED-007,
SRC-ONT-001 through SRC-ONT-004) and the specific datasets, tables, or records that
will be extracted from them for public display.

A single source may produce multiple datasets with different selections, fetch methods,
and Firestore targets — each gets its own Dataset ID (CV-DATA-xxx) in this plan.

This plan does not cover:
- US, UK, or Australian datasets — out of scope for the Canadian launch.
- Data that is generated by the app itself (e.g., citizen votes) — covered by CV-REG-002.
- Analytics or crash reporting data — covered by CV-REG-002 and CV-ISS-006/007.
- Future datasets not yet associated with a registered source in CV-REG-001.

---

## 3. Dataset Selection Principle

> **A dataset is not selected until it is explicitly named.**

The source decision (Approved with Limitations, Public Registry, etc.) in CV-REG-001
and CV-RPT-001 confirms that a source is *eligible* for use. It does not confirm that
a specific dataset from that source has been chosen for the app. These are two separate
decisions:

| Level | Decision | Who decides | Document |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source level | Is this source eligible, licensed, and attributed? | Data Lead | CV-REG-001 / CV-SRC-REV-xxx |
| Dataset level | Which specific table, file, indicator, or report from this source will be used? | Product Lead + Data Lead | **This plan (CV-PLAN-003)** |
| Record level | Has this specific data fetch been verified? | Data Lead (+ second reviewer for high-risk) | CV-REC-001 |

**Selection rules:**

1. A dataset may only be marked **Selected** or **Selected with Limitations** once the
   exact table name, dataset ID, file URL, or report title is identified.
2. A dataset may not be marked Selected if its reporting period is unknown.
3. A dataset may not be marked Selected if its Firestore collection is unknown.
4. A dataset may not be marked Selected if its app display location is unknown.
5. High-risk datasets (SRC-FED-005, 006, 007; SRC-ONT-003, 004) require a named second
   reviewer before their Decision Status can advance past Selected.
6. Manual Review Only datasets (SRC-FED-007, SRC-ONT-004) may not be batch-imported.
   Each record is individually selected, extracted, reviewed, and approved.
7. Datasets that cannot be selected for launch must be marked **Deferred** or
   **Not Approved** — not left as Candidate indefinitely.
8. Statistics Canada (SRC-FED-001) must remain at **Pending Product Decision** until
   the Founder / Product Lead confirms the exact indicator(s) and table number(s).

## 17. Verification Record Requirements

Before any dataset in this plan is written to Firestore:

1. A **CV-REC-001 Data Verification Checklist** must be completed for the specific
   dataset fetch event.
2. For high-risk datasets (CV-DATA-009, 011, 012, 015, 016), the second reviewer must
   sign off on the CV-REC-001 before the Firestore write proceeds.
3. For Manual Review Only datasets (CV-DATA-012, 016), a CV-REC-001 must be completed
   for **each individual record** — not once per fetch cycle.
4. The completed CV-REC-001 must be stored in `compliance/records/` with a filename
   identifying the dataset and fetch date (e.g., `CV-REC-001-CV-DATA-004-2025-09-15.md`).
5. CV-REG-001 must be updated with the fetch date and verification status after each
   completed CV-REC-001.
